Scripts

Word for word.
Use these exactly.

Don't improvise until you've made 50+ calls. Start here. Every line is tested.

STEP 01The Opener — 30 seconds max
"Hi, is this [Name]?

Hey, my name is [Your name] — I'm a web developer.

I help small businesses in the US get a professional website so they stop losing customers to competitors with a better online presence.

I noticed your business doesn't have one — or yours looks a little outdated.

Do you have 2 minutes?"
Say your name, one sentence about what you do, and ask for 2 minutes. That's it. Don't explain more until they say yes.
STEP 02Qualify and Pitch Fast
"Great. So basically what we do is build you a clean, professional website — home page, about, services, contact — fully set up, done in about 7 to 10 days.

We charge a flat fee of $800 for a standard site, or $1,500 if you want an online store where customers can buy directly from you.

Does that sound like something that could help your business?"
Lead with the $1,500 package. Most people don't know they want an online store until you mention it.
STEP 03Book the Zoom
"I'd love to show you some examples of sites we've built for businesses similar to yours.

Can I get 20 minutes on your calendar? I'll do a quick screen share, show you exactly what it would look like for your business, and give you a firm price — no obligation.

Are you free tomorrow or the day after?"
Always give two options — "tomorrow or the day after." One date creates friction. Two options creates a decision.
STEP 04The Zoom Close
"Thanks for your time today. So as you can see from the examples, we build clean, fast, professional sites that actually bring in customers.

For your business specifically, I'd recommend the [package name] — that comes to $[price], all in, done in [X] days.

We take 50% upfront to get started, and the rest when we hand it over.

Does that work for you?"
After they say yes: confirm package + price out loud, send payment link immediately, get 50% paid before work starts.
Objection Handling

Every pushback.
Every answer.

Objections are questions in disguise. Know these responses by heart.

I already have a website
That's great! Can I ask — when was it last updated? Most sites 3–5 years old aren't mobile-friendly. Google penalizes those. We do redesigns from $600 — worth a 15-minute look?
I'm not interested
Totally fair — can I ask why? Is it budget, or you just don't see the value? Listen first. If it's budget, offer $800. If genuinely not interested, thank them and move on.
How much does it cost?
Packages start at $800. I'd love to show you examples on a quick Zoom so I can give you an exact quote. Are you free tomorrow?
I need to think about it
Of course! What specifically do you need to think through — price, timing, or something else? I want to answer everything before we hang up.
Send me an email
Absolutely — what's the best email? Send your offer right after the call, then follow up by phone the next day. Don't just email and wait.
Where are you calling from?
We're a remote team working with US clients across the country — everything over email and Zoom. We've built sites for businesses in Texas, California, Florida.
I don't have the budget right now
Is it timing, or price? We do 50% upfront and 50% on delivery — so you're only paying $400–$750 to get started. Does that work better?
I need to talk to my partner first
Of course — would it help to do a quick Zoom with both of you together? That way you can decide together. When would work for both of you?
Can you do it cheaper?
Our pricing is already well below US agencies — they'd charge $5K–$10K for the same work. Starting at $800 with 2-week delivery is the best value out there.
I'm too busy right now
Totally get it — that's why most businesses get a website: so it works for them while they're busy. The setup takes nothing from you. Worth a 20-min Zoom?

FAQ
Questions every rep asks.

If you're wondering something, someone else already asked it. Here are all the answers.

Commission is paid out as soon as the client's deposit clears — same day or next day. You'll never wait more than 24 hours after a deal is confirmed and payment is received.
You earn 20% of the full deal price, paid out in two parts: 20% of the deposit when the client pays upfront, and 20% of the remaining balance when the final payment comes in.
If a client cancels before work starts and requests a refund, the commission is adjusted accordingly. If work has already started and no refund is issued, your commission stands in full.
Use the Zoom Close script in the Scripts section above. The structure is: quick intro → show 2–3 portfolio examples → recommend the right package → state the price confidently → ask for the sale → handle objections → send the payment link.
You'll be given portfolio examples to use before you start calling. Never promise custom mockups on a cold call — just tell them you have examples to show on the Zoom. The portfolio will be shared with you by the team lead.
After they agree on the Zoom, send them the payment link immediately. The best time to collect is while they're still excited — excitement fades fast. Don't wait until "later."
Life happens — but be honest in the daily check-in. The team lead is here to help, not punish. Consistency over the week matters more than any single day. If you're consistently under target, expect a 1-on-1 coaching call.

Pro Tips

What top reps
do differently.

These aren't tricks. They're habits that compound over time.

Always push for the $1,500 package

When someone says yes to a website, ask: "Would you also want customers to buy from you online? We can add a full store for $700 more." That extra $700 adds $140 to your commission on every deal.

Always offer the maintenance retainer

After every close: "We also have a $150/month maintenance plan — updates, security, everything running smoothly. Want to add that?" It's $30/month in your pocket for zero extra work.

Your voice is your most important tool

Stand up when you call. Smile — people can hear it. Talk calm and confident. A warm tone keeps people on the line longer than any script ever will.

Follow up — most closes happen on call 2

If someone said "call me Thursday" or asked for an email, follow up exactly on time. Reliability stands out. Most reps never follow up — that's your edge.

Track your own numbers

Keep a personal record: calls made, Zooms booked, deals closed, commission earned. Watching your numbers improve week over week is the best motivation there is.

Two nos = move on

If someone objects twice, thank them and end the call. Your time is valuable. 30 calls per day means you only need 1–2 yeses to have a great day. Protect your energy.
